DATA LICENCE - PhysioFlow datasets
==================================

This file covers the contents of `data/` only. The software itself is licensed
separately, under the GNU General Public License v2.0 or later; see the LICENSE
file at the root of the repository.

The folder holds material of three different origins. Rather than assert a
single licence over files the authors do not own, this file states the terms of
each part. `data-README.md` in the same folder documents every file
individually. The same two documents ship with the Code Ocean reproducible
capsule (DOI 10.24433/CO.9901925.v1), where Code Ocean asks for one data licence
for the whole /data folder.

1. Synthetic ecophysiology dataset
---------------------------------
`sample/physiology_synthetic.csv` is generated by the authors with a fixed seed
(`scripts/make_synthetic_physiology.py`). It contains no measurement: it
reproduces the reference schema and the structural properties discussed in the
article.

  The authors release this file under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  International licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2. Public datasets redistributed here
-------------------------------------
The files under `sample/test/` are public datasets redistributed unmodified for
the verification steps. They remain under the terms of their original sources,
and nothing here extends or restricts those terms:

  penguins.csv            Palmer Penguins (Gorman, Williams & Fraser; Palmer
                          Station LTER). Released by its authors under CC0 1.0.
  yates.oats.txt          Yates (1935) oats trial, as distributed with the R
                          package nlme; the dataset is long in the public
                          domain and is reproduced in the statistical
                          literature.
  OATS.csv                the same trial, comma-separated.
  sweetpotato.csv         the sweetpotato dataset of the R package agricolae
                          (CRAN), distributed under the GNU General Public
                          License.
  plantgrowth.csv         PlantGrowth, from the R `datasets` package (base R,
                          GPL-2 | GPL-3), after Dobson (1983).
  sk_crd1.csv             datasets shipped with the R package ScottKnott
  sk_rcbd.csv             (CRAN), which is distributed under the GNU General
  sk_sorghum.csv          Public License; sk_sorghum is the sorghum-yield
                          example of Jelihovschi, Faria & Allaman (2014).
  BESAG_ELBATAN.txt       datasets of the ASReml Cookbook, redistributed for
  SPRING_BARLEY.txt       academic use under the terms of their publisher.
  australia.soybean.txt

3. Material generated by the authors from the datasets above
------------------------------------------------------------
`sample/test/exemplo_faixas.csv` and `sample/test/exemplo_nested.csv` are
balanced trials generated by the authors to exercise the strip-plot and nested
designs. `sample/test/posthoc_reference_R.json` holds the reference values that
`scripts/gerar_referencia_posthoc_R.R` computes in R from the public datasets of
section 2; it records statistical results, not the data themselves.

  Released under CC BY 4.0, as in section 1. The terms of section 2 continue to
  apply to the datasets the reference values were computed from.

Restricted material
-------------------
The ecophysiology field campaign behind the figures of the article belongs to
an ongoing research project and is NOT part of this repository, under any
licence. It is excluded from version control, and no file here derives from its
measured values.

Citing
------
If you use the synthetic datasets, cite the software through CITATION.cff. If
you use the public datasets, cite their original sources as listed above and in
data-README.md.
